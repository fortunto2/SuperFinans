# Getting Started

SuperFinans helps you reach your financial goals with compound interest tracking, spending insights, and complete privacy.

## Create Your First Goal
![star.fill]
Tap the + button on the Goals tab to create a savings goal. Give it a name, set your target amount, and pick a target date. SuperFinans will calculate how much you need to save each month.

## Log Deposits
![plus.circle.fill]
Every time you put money toward your goal, log it as a deposit. Tap your goal, then "Add Deposit" to record the amount. Your progress ring will update instantly.

## Track Your Progress
![chart.line.uptrend.xyaxis]
Watch your progress ring fill up as you save. You'll hit milestones at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% with celebration animations to keep you motivated.

## Explore Projections
![chart.xyaxis.line]
The projection chart shows how your savings will grow over time, including compound interest. Use the "What If" slider to see how changing your monthly contribution affects your target date.
