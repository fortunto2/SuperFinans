# Goals

Goals are the heart of SuperFinans. Each goal tracks a specific savings target with optional compound interest.

## Creating a Goal
![plus.circle.fill]
Tap + on the Goals tab. Enter a name (like "Emergency Fund" or "Vacation"), your target amount, and an optional target date. Pick an icon and color to make it yours.

## Compound Interest
![percent]
If your savings earn interest (like a HYSA or investment account), enter the annual interest rate. SuperFinans calculates compound growth using your chosen frequency: monthly, quarterly, or annually.

## Monthly Contribution
![calendar.badge.plus]
Set how much you plan to save each month. SuperFinans shows whether you're on track to hit your target date, and suggests adjustments if you're behind.

## What-If Slider
![slider.horizontal.3]
On the goal detail screen, drag the What-If slider to explore different monthly contribution amounts. See in real time how your target date shifts.

## Milestones
![flag.fill]
Every goal has four milestones: 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100%. When you hit one, you'll see a celebration animation. Stay motivated by watching your progress grow.

## Deposits & Withdrawals
![arrow.up.arrow.down]
Log every deposit or withdrawal against your goal. Each entry records the amount, date, and an optional note. Your current balance and progress ring update automatically.

## Reordering Goals
![arrow.up.arrow.down.circle]
Long-press and drag goals in the list to reorder them by priority. Your most important goal stays at the top.
