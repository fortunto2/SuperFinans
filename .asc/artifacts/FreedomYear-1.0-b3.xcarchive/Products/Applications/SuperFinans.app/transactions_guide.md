# Transactions

Track your income and expenses to understand where your money goes and find more to save.

## Adding a Transaction
![plus.circle.fill]
Tap + on the Transactions tab. The numpad appears first for fast amount entry. Then pick a category, date, and optionally add a note.

## Categories
![square.grid.2x2.fill]
SuperFinans includes 20 built-in categories like Groceries, Dining, Housing, Transportation, and more. Each has its own icon and color for easy scanning.

## Income vs Expenses
![arrow.up.arrow.down]
Positive amounts are income, negative are expenses. Your running balance updates automatically per account.

## Accounts
![building.columns.fill]
Organize transactions across up to 3 accounts in the free tier (unlimited with Premium). Create checking, savings, credit card, or cash accounts.

## Date Grouping
![calendar]
Transactions are grouped by date so you can easily see daily spending. Navigate between months to review past activity.

## Recurring Transactions
![repeat]
Set up templates for regular transactions like rent, salary, or subscriptions. SuperFinans tracks the schedule and reminds you when they're due.
