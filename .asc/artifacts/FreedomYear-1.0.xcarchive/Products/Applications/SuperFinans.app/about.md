# About SuperFinans

A private, offline-first savings tracker that puts your goals first.

## Why SuperFinans?
![heart.fill]
Most finance apps focus on budgeting and charge yearly subscriptions. SuperFinans focuses on savings goals with compound interest, works offline, respects your privacy, and costs a one-time $29.99.

## Who It's For
![person.2.fill]
Families tired of $100+/year finance app subscriptions. Young professionals building their first emergency fund. Privacy-conscious savers who don't want their financial data in the cloud.

## How It's Built
![hammer.fill]
SuperFinans is built with SwiftUI and Core Data. All data stays on your device with optional iCloud sync. No servers, no databases, no analytics — just your iPhone.
