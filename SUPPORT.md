# Support — Freedom Year

Something broken, something confusing, or a number that looks wrong? Write to
[info@superduperai.co](mailto:info@superduperai.co) — a person reads it.

For a bug, the useful details are: what you tapped, what you expected, what happened, plus
the app version from Settings → About.

## Questions that come up

**Where does the 7% come from?** It is a long-run figure for a broadly diversified
dollar-denominated stock market, after inflation but before tax. It is an assumption, not a
promise, and you can change it. If your plan is denominated in a currency with high
inflation, the app says so and offers to restate the plan in dollars — a nominal 7% in a
currency losing 15% a year is arithmetic that lies.

**How is the year calculated?** The app compounds your invested savings month by month,
adds your monthly contribution, and stops at the first month where the pot's monthly yield
covers a month of your spending. That is the same idea as the 4% rule, expressed as a date
instead of a multiple.

**Why does it ask so little?** Because everything else can be derived or refined later.
Children, household size and lifestyle are already inside the number you gave for monthly
spending — asking for them separately would be asking twice.

**Do I have to enter transactions?** No. Accounts and transactions exist for people who
want the estimate to track reality on its own, but the answer works without them.

**Is my financial data uploaded?** No. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Data export

Settings → Export Data writes everything the app holds as JSON, readable without this app.
