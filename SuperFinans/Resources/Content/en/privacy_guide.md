# Privacy & Security

Your financial data is personal. SuperFinans is designed to keep it that way.

## Offline-First
![wifi.slash]
SuperFinans works completely offline. No internet connection required to create goals, log transactions, or view insights. Your data is stored on your device.

## No Tracking
![eye.slash.fill]
SuperFinans contains zero analytics, zero ad trackers, and zero third-party SDKs that collect your data. We don't know what you earn, spend, or save.

## iCloud Sync
![icloud.fill]
Your data syncs across your Apple devices through iCloud using your personal Apple ID. The sync uses Apple's encrypted CloudKit infrastructure — we never see your data.

## On-Device AI
![brain]
AI insights (Premium) run entirely on your iPhone using Apple's Foundation Models. Your financial data is never sent to any server for AI processing.

## Export Your Data
![square.and.arrow.up]
You own your data. Export everything as JSON anytime from Settings. No lock-in, no restrictions.
