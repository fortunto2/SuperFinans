# Freedom Year

**When can you stop working?**

Three numbers — what life costs each month, what you have invested, what you add
each month — and the app returns the year your investments start covering your
life instead of your salary. No bank login, no month of collecting receipts
before it tells you anything.

Every other money app answers *where did last month go*. This one answers *when
does this end*.

## What it does

- **The year, and your age in it.** Not a progress bar — a date you can argue with.
- **A what-if slider.** What another $100 a month is worth, measured in years.
- **Life events.** Children moving out, a mortgage ending, a pension starting.
  Spending is not flat for thirty years, and modelling it as flat is the biggest
  lie in most FIRE calculators.
- **Gold by the gram, and savings in any currency.** Turkey keeps savings in gold;
  so do India and the Gulf. 156 currencies, valued at live rates.
- **A home screen widget** with the year on it, so the goal is not buried in an
  app you forget to open.
- **One notification a month.** Not a daily nag to log a coffee.

## Honest about the arithmetic

A 7% return is a dollar-market figure. If your plan is denominated in a currency
that runs high inflation, the app says so instead of quietly producing a wrong
year, and offers to restate the plan in dollars at the current rate.

## Private by construction

Not one amount you type leaves your phone. No account, no ads, no third parties,
nothing sold. The arithmetic runs on the device; iCloud sync, if you use it, runs
through your own account. An anonymous launch counter is the only thing sent, and
Settings turns it off.
